Shamisen samples extracted from gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts (instrument: shamisen, notes A2-G#6)

musyngkite/ : Musyng Kite soundfont - Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0
fatboy/     : FatBoy soundfont - Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0
fluidr3/    : FluidR3_GM soundfont by Frank Wen - Creative Commons Attribution 3.0

The per-note "cents" values in manifest.json were measured for this app
to correct the tuning of each recording.
